What we collect

  • Account: your email address, when you joined and confirmed your email, and the price you agreed to.
  • Billing: handled by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer and subscription IDs and status — never your card number.
  • Your Crown Tools data (profiles, orders, inboxes, settings): encrypted on your device with your password before it reaches us. We store only the encrypted copy and cannot read it.
  • Technical: the IP address of requests (for security and abuse prevention), and when you last used the app.

Email sync

When you connect an inbox, your device sends the login for that inbox to our server only while a sync is running, so the server can read order emails and send the results back to your device. We don’t keep your inbox password or your emails on our server outside of your encrypted data.

How we use it

To run your account, take payments, send account emails (confirmations, password resets, billing notices), prevent abuse, and keep Crown Tools working. We don’t sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who we share with

Only service providers that run Crown Tools: Stripe (payments), Vercel (hosting), Upstash (storage), our email provider, and shipping carriers when you look up tracking numbers. They may only use it to provide their service.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your account while it exists. Deleting your account (Settings → Account) erases your account, encrypted data and online backups right away; Stripe keeps billing records as the law requires.

Security

Your data is encrypted with AES-256 using a key made from your password on your device. Connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but we’ve designed Crown Tools so a breach of our servers would not reveal your profiles or orders.

Your choices

You can download, correct or delete your data anytime in the app, or email [support email]. If you’re in a place with privacy laws (like California or the EU), you have the rights those laws give you; contact us to use them.
